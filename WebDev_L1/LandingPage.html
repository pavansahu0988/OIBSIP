What's inside

Four things, chosen to run out together.

Every box is built around one roll of film, so the chemistry and paper inside are sized to match — nothing left over, nothing missing.

One roll of film

A different stock each month — black and white or color, picked for the season and explained in the insert card.

Developer & fixer

Pre-measured for a single roll, in resealable pouches — mix what you need and store the rest for next time.

Printing paper

A small pack of resin-coated paper, enough for a contact sheet and a handful of test prints.

A reading card

Notes on the film's character, a starting exposure, and a development time for the chemistry that shipped with it.
